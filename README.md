# Lucky-Namakeen-Management-System
Created a professional Google Sheets-based system to manage customer payments, delivery tracking, and supplier payment records for Lucky Namakeen business. Designed a centralized dashboard to display all business data in real time, improving financial visibility, reducing manual errors, and helping the owner manage operations efficiently.


📖 Project Overview

The Lucky Namakeen Business Management System is a Google Sheets-based solution designed to manage daily business operations, including customer payments, delivery tracking, and supplier payments. The system provides a centralized dashboard for real-time monitoring of business data and financial performance.

🎯 Problem Statement

Lucky Namakeen was managing business records manually, which caused:

Payment tracking errors

Supplier dues confusion

No real-time business insights

Difficulty in tracking deliveries and performance

A digital and automated system was required to improve accuracy and efficiency.

🛠️ Solution

I designed a structured Google Sheets Management System with multiple modules:

✅ Payment Management

Customer payment tracking

Pending and completed payment status

Summary of daily, weekly, and monthly collections

✅ Delivery Tracking

Delivery date, customer name, product details, and quantity

Delivery status monitoring (Delivered / Pending)

Performance tracking for logistics

✅ Supplier Payment Management

Supplier-wise purchase and payment records

Outstanding dues calculation

Payment reconciliation

✅ Business Dashboard

Real-time financial and operational dashboard

Total sales, expenses, and profit overview

Receivables and payables summary

Interactive charts and filters

📊 Key Features

Centralized business data management

Automated calculations using formulas and pivot tables

Real-time dashboards and visual analytics

Easy-to-use structure for non-technical business owners

Scalable system for future business growth

🚀 Impact & Results

Reduced manual accounting errors by up to 80%

Improved business decision-making with real-time insights

Saved several hours of manual data entry and calculations

Increased transparency in supplier and customer transactions
